Read the curb before you park.

Search official NYC DOT parking-sign records for the Bronx and Queens. Each sign comes with a plain-English reading so you know what to look for.

How it works

1. You search

Type a street. The app turns it into a question for NYC Open Data and asks for matching records.

2. The city answers

Records come straight from NYC DOT's Parking Regulation Locations and Signs dataset. Nothing is made up or stored.

3. We explain it

Each sign's official text is shown as written. A short plain-English line is added using NYC DOT's published sign legend.

Limits you should know

Records can be out of date. Temporary rules, new signs, and removed signs may not appear. The plain-English line is a general guide, not a legal reading. Signs with exceptions, times, or special conditions must be read in full on the street. Currently the app covers the Bronx and Queens only.

Report a problem

Missing, damaged, or confusing signs? Write it down here. CurbLens does not send reports. Notes are saved only in this browser. To file an official complaint, use 311.
